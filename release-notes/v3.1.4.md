### New
- **Automation tab** for every server: start when PackCheck opens, restart after a crash (limited per hour; a manual or forced stop is respected), scheduled restart with a countdown in chat, scheduled backup, scheduled or repeating commands, chat announcements, warn or restart on low TPS or high memory, and stop when the server has been empty for a while. Choose days of the week, run any rule now, see the next and last run, and follow everything in the activity log. "Recommended setup" adds the usual rules in one click.
- **Assistant modes**: *Ask before changes* (default), *Plan* (looks around and writes a plan; nothing changes until you press Run the plan), *Auto-edit files* (file edits apply at once with a backup; commands and start/stop still ask) and *Bypass* (everything without asking, after a confirmation and with a red banner).
