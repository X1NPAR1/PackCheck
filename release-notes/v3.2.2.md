PackCheck 3.2.2 – the in-app update no longer gets stuck at 100 %.

### Fixed
- **Updating could stop at 100 %**: after the download, PackCheck asked whether to close ports that were open to the internet. That question kept the app open while the updater waited, so after 60 seconds the update gave up and the window stayed at 100 %. During an update no exit questions are asked now (open ports stay open, so the server is reachable again right after the update).
- The updater waits up to 3 minutes for PackCheck to close and then ends it instead of giving up, so an update always finishes.
- After the download the dialog says what happens next: verifying, stopping servers, starting the installer.

If an older version is stuck at 100 %: close PackCheck from the tray menu (answer the ports question if it appears), then download `PackCheck-Setup-3.2.2.exe` below and run it. Your servers and settings are kept.
